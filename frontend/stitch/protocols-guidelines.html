Operational Protocols & Safety Guidelines

Mandatory procedures and reference documentation for Sector 7G field operations.

warning Relocation Protocol 12-B

HIGH-RISK ZONE
01. Perimeter Establishment expand_more
Secure a 500-meter perimeter around the affected zone. Deploy hazard markers and restrict civilian access immediately. Ensure all access points are monitored by authorized personnel.
02. Initial Assessment & Triage expand_more
Conduct rapid visual assessment. Classify structural integrity using standard metrics. Prioritize evacuation for individuals with medical dependencies.
03. Extraction Procedures expand_more
Initiate staggered extraction. Utilize designated safe routes only. Maintain continuous communication with the Strategic Hub during transport.
