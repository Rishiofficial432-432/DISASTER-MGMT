Protocols & Safety Guidelines

Reference procedures for relocation prioritisation and field response, sourced from official Indian government disaster-management documents.

calculate Priority Scoring Methodology

Each settlement receives a score 0–6 combining hazard exposure (0–4 pts) and population density tier (0–2 pts). High scores indicate the most urgent relocation need.

emergency Relocation Decision Protocol
  1. 1 Identify settlements inside a mapped hazard zone using the point-in-polygon exposure check. Any settlement inside a high-severity zone is automatically high priority.
  2. 2 Classify residential density against URDPFI 2014/2015 norms (75–125 persons/hectare for small-town, plain terrain). Hill terrain carries an additional 0.5× multiplier.
  3. 3 Combine hazard severity and density tier into a priority score (0–6). Settlements scoring ≥ 4 are flagged as high priority.
  4. 4 Review high-priority settlements first for detailed field assessment. Do not initiate relocation based solely on this tool — verify with district disaster management authorities.
  5. 5 Document all field findings and update the settlement record with drone-measured areas before the final report.
campaign Communication Strategy
  • Use plain-language verdicts (URGENT / WATCH / SAFE) for field officers and residents — the "Check My Village" tool is designed for this.
  • Always disclose data limitations to end users. This prototype uses demonstration hazard data, not a live NDMA/NRSC feed.
  • Escalate high-priority settlements to district disaster management authorities for verification before any action.
  • Maintain a log of all searches and verdicts served for accountability and audit trail.
  • Provide results in the local language (Assamese) for affected communities in production deployment.
