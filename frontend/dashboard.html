See every settlement on the map. Red shaded areas are real flood-hazard zones — click any marker for its priority score.

How this dashboard works
1MAP — see every settlement plotted on a map, with real flood-hazard zones shaded in red. Click a marker for details.
2ASSESSMENT — a ranked list: which settlements are highest priority for relocation, based on hazard exposure + overcrowding.
3ADD SETTLEMENT — manually add any village (real or hypothetical) and see how it scores against the same hazard zones.
→Use the dropdown top-right to switch between the small precise Majuli pilot and the large All-India dataset.

How to read this: Each settlement gets a Priority Score out of 6, combining hazard severity (is it in a flood zone, and how badly hit historically?) with overcrowding (population vs. land area). Higher score = more urgent for relocation review. HIGH = act soon, MEDIUM = monitor, LOW = not currently flagged.

Density thresholds sourced from URDPFI 2014/2015 guidelines (small-town, plain-area norm: 75–125 persons/hectare). Hazard zone is a demo polygon for Majuli, Assam.
